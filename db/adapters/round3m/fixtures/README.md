# Round 3M live fixture receipts

`live_fixture_receipts.json` contains only field labels, public source
locators, retrieval timestamps, hashes, expected classifications, and counts.
It deliberately contains no third-party descriptor text.

The official source publishers are the Alliance for Coffee Excellence / Cup
of Excellence, World Coffee Championships / the Busan Coffee Championship,
and the UC Davis Library World Coffee Championships collection. Their public
pages and artifacts remain under their respective terms. Public availability
is not treated as permission to republish or use descriptors for modeling.
The project has not modified or redistributed those source artifacts.

The CoE hashes identify the governed Round 3L route/index snapshots. Those
indexes establish the edition and detail-page routes but do not contain the
descriptor-bearing detail-page bodies. The scope field records that limitation
explicitly. The WCC negative hashes identify complete blank-form or ranking
artifacts. Assertions from an unstored detail body must remain hash-only,
provisional, non-model-eligible evidence until the body is lawfully stored and
its own checksum is verified.
