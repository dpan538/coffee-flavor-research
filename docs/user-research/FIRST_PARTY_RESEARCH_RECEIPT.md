## Framing

- Expert research validates the domain: the vocabulary system, the problem
  definition and the product logic.
- Pre-launch tasting tests validate the product experience under different
  extraction and drinking conditions.
- Neither is a general-audience sample; the project does not claim to have
  validated ordinary-consumer demand.

## Boundaries

- The app collects no interaction data; nothing leaves the device.
- Counts above are the owner's report and are marked OWNER_REPORTED in the
  public claims register.
