# Known gaps

- The held-out normalization cases are project-authored, not independent
  ordinary-user observations; C0/C1 normalization sufficiency remains false.
- The imported sensory rows occupy only batch-filter × medium roast, so
  preparation, roast, and interaction sensory effects are not estimable.
- There are no imported milk-coffee sensory outcomes.
- The candidate DOI `10.5061/dryad.v15dv423h` lacks a publicly viewable exact
  version/files/license record and was not imported.
- Chinese terminology review supports the seven-level product vocabulary but
  does not establish universal synonyms, boundaries, or nationwide usage.
- Source concentration is high (maximum share 0.6614), and geographic fields
  are not a representative sample of coffee consumers or production regions.
- No embedding baseline, pgvector dependency, Q1–Q5 interaction, frontend
  context UI, or production sensory coefficient exists.

Next recommended phase: independent bilingual ordinary-user comprehension and
label-resolution evaluation plus a rights-cleared sensory dataset with varied,
replicated preparation × roast cells and meaningful milk coverage.
