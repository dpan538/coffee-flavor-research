# Source, rights, and access audit

## Decision rule

Official public access is recorded separately from permission. Each source
requires independent decisions for public results use, public descriptor use,
internal research use, public derived release, model research use, and
commercial model use. Unknown or request-required dimensions never become
affirmative through inference.

The authoritative matrices contain 109 access rows and 12 prepared organizer
request rows. Every request is `NOT_SENT`. No form was submitted, no email sent,
no export requested, no purchase made, and no contract or terms accepted.

show an explicit grant for the six Round 3K uses.

CoE records are credited.

is unavailable, and zero Best of Panama records are credited.
