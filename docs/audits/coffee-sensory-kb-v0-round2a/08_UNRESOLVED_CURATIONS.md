# Round 2A unresolved curations

Date: 2026-08-24

Status: intentionally unresolved inventory; not a runtime failure

Round 2A records uncertainty instead of filling the ontology to a visual or
numeric quota. The following decisions remain intentionally unresolved for a
later evidence review.

## Candidate concepts

- `sensory.pink_grapefruit` remains distinct from `sensory.grapefruit`, but is
  a candidate. Targeted research did not locate a coffee-specific formal
  lexicon or peer-reviewed coffee study strong enough to justify active status.
  Commercial tasting-note occurrences were not treated as canonical evidence.
- `sensory.blackcurrant`, `sensory.orange_blossom`, `sensory.mint`,
  `sensory.eucalyptus`, `sensory.lemongrass`, `sensory.green_tea`, and
  `sensory.leather` remain candidates. Exact use was not verified in the
  checked Bollen article text, and no exact-term supplementary evidence was
  verified for these seven identities by 2026-08-24. This is a bounded,
  non-exhaustive review result, not a claim that no relevant evidence can exist
  elsewhere.
- `qualifier.bright`, `qualifier.clean`, `qualifier.juicy`,
  `qualifier.tea_like`, `qualifier.winey`, and `qualifier.jammy` remain
  candidates. None has an intrinsic formula, score, vector, or projection
  coordinate.
- The shared English expression “winey” retains two explicit possible senses:
  an active sensory character and a candidate qualifier. Retrieval does not
  force the ambiguous surface form to one concept.

## Deliberate non-admissions

Round 2A does not activate umbrella or meta terms merely to reproduce a source
wheel or make a balanced diagram. Examples retained only in the curation record
include `fruity`, `floral`, `nutty`, `sweet aromatic`, `sour aromatic`,
`amplitude`, `overall impact`, `blended`, and `longevity`.

Chemistry-adjacent labels such as `butyric acid`, `isovaleric acid`, `malic
acid`, `citric acid`, and `vanillin` were not collapsed into familiar sensory
references. Their perceptual and chemical boundaries require a separate review.
Culture- or context-sensitive leaves such as `shoyu`, `umami`, `fishy`,
`animalic`, `meaty/brothy`, `whiskey`, `rum`, and `liqueur` likewise remain
outside the canonical core until their scope and evidence can be assessed
without importing a protected source list.

## Partial WCR projection

The WCR 2.0 source scheme is intentionally partial and flat. It contains only
the 24 labels that WCR itself enumerates on its public resource page, no copied
definitions, references, intensities, or hierarchy. Fifteen nodes have reviewed
canonical mappings; nine remain deliberately unmapped:

- Butyric acid
- Isovaleric acid
- Fresh
- Musty/Earthy
- Musty/Dusty
- Moldy/Damp
- Brown Spice
- Vanillin
- Floral

An unmapped source node is not an error. It records that Round 2A declined to
assert equivalence or a broader/narrower correspondence.

## Deferred work

- Comprehensive Simplified Chinese lexicalization remains a later curation
  round. The schema and `zh-Hans` language registry remain operational.
- Composite consumer references remain intentionally small. Round 2A does not
  import food, confectionery, cocktail, or roaster-marketing vocabularies.
- Industry-language acquisition and unresolved roaster terms belong to Round
  2B. No Round 2A fixture is represented as an industry corpus.
- Empirical PCA, MDS, correspondence-analysis, or factor coordinates remain in
  the evidence projection domain when a lawful dataset is available; no
  permanent concept coordinates were added.

## Rights follow-up

The applicable reuse licence for the Ledezma article remains unverified. Its
source version is therefore metadata-only with every reuse and production
export permission closed. Before any source-content use, a future review must
verify the exact version and applicable terms; current bibliographic support
does not authorize copied text, tables, or supplementary data.

Open-access article metadata likewise remains evidence-only in V0 even where a
CC BY 4.0 licence was verified. This deliberate production-export restriction
may be revisited only through a version-specific rights and product-scope
decision, never by assuming that public access is sufficient.

## Promotion rule

No candidate is promoted merely because it appears in industry language, makes
the hierarchy visually balanced, or helps meet a target count. Promotion
requires a source-versioned evidence review, a distinct independently authored
scope, rights-safe provenance, preferred-label review, and successful semantic
validation. Source-local labels may instead remain unmapped when equivalence is
unsafe.
