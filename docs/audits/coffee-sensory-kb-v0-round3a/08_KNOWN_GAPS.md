# Known gaps

- The target-user comprehension of eight preparation families is not yet tested.
- The five roast labels are a product interaction hypothesis, not calibrated universal categories.
- Direct sensory evidence comparing Americano and long black is insufficient.
- Flat-white/latte recipe labels vary; the sibling design preserves information without asserting universal ratios.
- AeroPress remains recipe-sensitive despite its useful multi-parent representation.
- Nitro cold brew has a smaller evidence base than ordinary cold and hot preparations.
- The Firstbloom pilot has zero structured preparation and roast coverage.
- The current lexical bridge contains two unresolved preparation labels and thirteen unresolved roast labels.
- City+, other traditional labels, regional methods, and additional alternative milks need source-specific review.
- The three Dryad candidates have not been imported or independently row-counted from frozen files.
- Preparation × roast interactions are represented conceptually but no coefficient is estimated.
- Black- versus milk-coffee conditional ranking has not been trained or evaluated.
- No Q1–Q5 work, ranking, embedding, pgvector, personalization, API, or frontend context control is included.

These gaps are explicit constraints on the next phase. They are not permission to infer missing corpus metadata or normalize ambiguous terminology automatically.
