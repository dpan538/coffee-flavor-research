## Evidence limitations

Cotter supplies 3,186 of the 3,289 qualifying evaluation documents, so document
volume is not balanced family coverage. Firstbloom contributes broad lexical
depth from one historical industry origin. The two Chinese sources close the
hard family gate but do not represent all regions, professional registers, or
cultural meanings and do not establish preferred translations.

Sensory measurements remain source-local. CATA, JAR, RATA, trained-panel,
Q-grader, liking, and prose observations are not interchangeable. The new
acidity--citrus result is correlational for one coffee and preparation design;
all range lifecycles remain `CANDIDATE`.

Twelve question targets have research support, but user validation and
information-gain counts remain zero. The 20 features and 12 partitions are
prebuild registries, not a trained model, calibrated policy, embedding space,
or executed train/test split.

Some sources permit public derivatives while raw rows or metadata remain
external or excluded. Complete rights review does not mean whole-source
republication.

## Operational closure

The manifest hash and two-rebuild comparison now pass. The exact candidate SHA,
final candidate CI, main promotion, main CI, and annotated tag are still
pending. These are release engineering gates, not data-coverage gaps. Any
failure keeps
`RESEARCH_DATABASE_V0_FREEZE_READY=false` and must be reported under the
corresponding blocked state.

Future evidence should enter a new v0.2 or later release through forward
migrations and a new manifest; it must not mutate v0.1.0 in place.
