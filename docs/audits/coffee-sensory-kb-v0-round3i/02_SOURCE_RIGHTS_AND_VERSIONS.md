# Source rights and versions

Six language-source records are admitted. They represent six canonical
origins, of which five are new countable Round 3I family gains: three
contemporary evaluation families and two source-authored Simplified-Chinese
families. Firstbloom is the pre-existing historical language origin. The
Cotter relationship record is a role-specific reuse of the same Dryad origin,
not a seventh independent family.

Each row separately records raw-text internal use, raw-text public
redistribution, derived-expression internal/public use, derived-count
internal/public use, and model-research use. A permissive source license does
not override the narrower project export scope or privacy exclusions.

All six language records have a non-empty exact version, stable URL,
repository, license basis, completed rights review, completed privacy review,
file-manifest hashes, language/geography/type/role fields, limitations, and an
admitted lifecycle. The Cotter relationship derivative publishes 27 condition
aggregates; its pseudonymous raw consumer field remains external to that public
artifact. Its separate role-specific accounting is 3,186 raw, 3,186 admitted,
and zero excluded consumer-tasting rows under unit
`source_consumer_evaluation_row`; this is reuse of the same Dryad origin, not an
additional independent source family.

Migration 048 declares one explicit row-accounting unit for the language
registry: `reviewed_candidate_sensory_occurrence_or_cell`. It records
raw/admitted/excluded counts of 54,162/10,763/43,399 for Cotter, 760/530/230 for
Bollen, 152/151/1 for Vezzulli, 4,827/1,058/3,769 for Firstbloom, 230/230/0 for
Junru Zhang, and 23/23/0 for Rinzemoon. These are reviewed candidate
occurrences or cells, not physical-file rows or language-document counts; those
separate units remain in the source annotation matrix.

The candidate register contains 10 named candidates. Open Food Facts remains
pending a fixed snapshot and governed partition; RoastDB, LoffeeLabs, and CQI
mirrors are not admitted because visibility or mirrors do not establish
upstream rights and independence. Non-admission contributes no family,
document, or expression count.
