# Label and split health

The professional pilot has 140 admitted provisional assertions, 137
record-unique assertions, eight effective records, one source family, and zero
reviewed/model-eligible normalized labels. The five P2 multi-descriptor records
generate 508 pair events, but their descriptor identities remain source-text
hashes.

Coffee-identity, participant, source-family, and year grouping rules are encoded
in the split audit. A grouped train/validation/test split is not feasible, and
there is no held-out family. The failed split result is an audit pass: the
system correctly refuses to invent a healthy manifest.
