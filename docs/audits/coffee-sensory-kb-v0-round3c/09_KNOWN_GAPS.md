# Known gaps

- No public dataset is sufficient for the intended calibration function.
- Institutional human-participant approval is not obtained.
- Final bilingual consent and public-release authorization are not ready.
- No real calibration beverage, assessor, response, or sensory observation was
  created.
- Minimum-pilot power and production split performance are not established.
- Context effects, preparation effects, roast effects, milk effects,
  interactions, question information gains, stopping thresholds, prior
  strength, override rate, and candidate-ranking performance are all
  `NOT_ESTIMABLE`.
- Question prompts are project-authored draft instruments and require cognitive
  interviewing and independent bilingual review.
- Deep learning, embeddings, pgvector, production ranking, and frontend
  integration are intentionally absent.

These are scientific/governance boundaries, not failed engineering gates.
