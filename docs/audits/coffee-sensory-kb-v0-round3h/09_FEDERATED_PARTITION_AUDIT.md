# Federated-partition audit

Twelve source partitions are declared. Four represent baseline data, five
represent Round 3H sensory outcomes, and three are metadata-only instrument
partitions. Every partition names eligibility, method semantics, grouping
keys, and available features.

No partition is currently model-authorized. Eligible outcome partitions are
conditional on a separately approved future protocol; the FT-NIR partition is
ineligible and instrument partitions remain metadata-only.
