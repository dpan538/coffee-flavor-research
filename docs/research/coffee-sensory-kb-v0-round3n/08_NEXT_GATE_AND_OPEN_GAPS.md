# Next gate and open gaps

The next round should be a human-reviewed product-task benchmark and policy
validation round, not automatically a training round.

Required evidence is: owner decisions for the 100-item packet; reviewed
source-to-C1 mappings; ordinary-user bilingual comprehension and response data;
counterfactual question assignment sufficient to estimate separation value;
held-out comparison of fixed versus adaptive questioning; product-deployment
rights; and a preregistered interpretation of candidate usefulness and
abstention.

Unsupported capabilities remain production question selection, calibrated
probabilities, scientific 5 + 3 validation, universal roast inference,
preparation inference from note style, deployment authorization, and any model
training claim.
