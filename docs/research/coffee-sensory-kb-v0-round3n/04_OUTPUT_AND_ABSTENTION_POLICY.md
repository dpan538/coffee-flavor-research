# Output and abstention policy

The simulator returns up to five main and three secondary references. It does
not force-fill slots. Main and secondary outputs require explicit answer
support, direct evidence, governed mapping, the public-research rights flag,
and survival of a one-per-redundancy-group filter.

The 120 fixtures include six 5 + 3 results, 34 partial results, 24 complete
abstentions, and 56 context-only `NEEDS_MANDATORY_Q1` states. Context-only
inputs do not create precise outputs.

Abstention rules cover rights blocks, weak direct support, unresolved mapping,
review-only relations, conflicting answers, missing C0/C1 evidence, close
boundaries, unknown/open-set expressions, fewer than five main outputs, and
missing secondary support. A partial or abstained output is valid behavior.
