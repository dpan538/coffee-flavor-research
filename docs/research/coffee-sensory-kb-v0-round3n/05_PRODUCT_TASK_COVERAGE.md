rows for descriptor family, question axis, language, evidence structure,
rights, review, and roast evidence.

source-native roast label has a reviewed project-C1 mapping. Three C0 families
have weak source-local Honey/Caramel evidence; the other five are neutral.

converted into negative evidence or a guessed roast prior.

that cross more preparation families, ordinary-user question validation, and
