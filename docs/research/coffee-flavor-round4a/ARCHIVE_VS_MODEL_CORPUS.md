# Archive versus model corpus

Lane A is the longitudinal archive. It can retain ranking-only, score-only,
metadata-only, protocol-only, missing, blocked, and descriptor-bearing years.
Its role is historical completeness.

Lane B contains bounded, coffee-specific professional sensory observations with
effective identity `series × edition × category × round × entry/lot ×
preparation service`. Judge rows, rankings, scores, awards, blank forms,
repeated publication, and mirrors do not become independent model records.

Lane C is the auxiliary language bridge. It supports retrieval and explanation
but cannot create professional truth.

The archive has 26,531 parsed rows. The descriptor lane has eight effective
records, 140 provisional assertions, 508 pair events, and zero model-eligible
assertions. Archive completeness therefore says nothing by itself about model
corpus completeness.
