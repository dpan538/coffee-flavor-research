# Task-specific data health

The compatibility audit covers professional normalization, source-local
classification, consumer-language mapping, context-conditioned retrieval, 5+3
ranking, co-assertion estimation, adaptive question selection, and stopping.
Every dataset-task cell records observation unit, raw and effective counts,
label and source, family concentration, language/context coverage, missingness,
duplicates, rights/review regimes, and split feasibility.

No empirical task passes. The professional candidate tasks have eight
effective records, one source family, zero reviewed/model-eligible labels, and
no feasible grouped train/validation/test split. Language corpora remain useful
for reference retrieval but are not task labels. Behavioral tasks contain zero
sessions.

The decision is based on effective labels, co-assertions, multi-target records,
and independent families—not raw archive volume.
