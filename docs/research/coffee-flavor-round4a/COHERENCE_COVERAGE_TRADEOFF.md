# Coherence–coverage trade-off

The evaluation is Pareto-based across Reference Coverage@8, nDCG@8,
reference-supported candidate rate, unsupported candidate rate, isolated
outlier rate, candidate-set coherence, and redundancy. The graph layer and
grouped split must accompany every value.

The central future comparison is independent-label ranker D versus D plus
professional coherence E. A visually cohesive set is not evidence of success.
Coherence gain with meaningful reference-coverage loss, synonym collapse, or
suppression of rare direct evidence fails the hypothesis.

All professional held-out metrics are currently `NOT_EVALUATED` because zero
normalized professional assertions are model eligible and no independent
family holdout exists.
