# Professional co-assertion graph

`G_professional` contains 508 governed pair events aggregated to 506 unique
hash-bound pairs. The events originate from five P2 descriptor-bearing
effective records in one source family and one edition year. Of the unique
pairs, 504 have effective-record support one and two have support two.

Each edge retains raw support, distinct effective-record support, source-family
support, year support, preparation scope, roast scope, publication layer,
evidence tier, rights regime, and review state. A repeated term within a record,
judge row, or publication mirror cannot add independent support.

The descriptor atoms remain hash-bound and provisionally classified. They are
therefore reference-only and are not silently mapped to public canonical
candidates. Multi-family, multi-year, preparation-specific, and roast-specific
candidate-level coverage are currently zero or unresolved.
