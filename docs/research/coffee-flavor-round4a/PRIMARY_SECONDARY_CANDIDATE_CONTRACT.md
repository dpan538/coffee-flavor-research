# Primary and secondary candidate contract

The first five candidates are the high-confidence core. Each must have high
individual relevance, direct Q support, direct professional support, or a
strong connection to another primary candidate. A primary with neither
connection nor direct evidence is an unsupported outlier.

The three secondary positions hold supported adjacent alternatives, a second
plausible branch, a more specific/general reference, or moderate-evidence
explanatory candidates. Each secondary connects to a primary or has strong
direct answer/professional support. Secondary positions are not filler.

The receipt records connected components, largest-component share, isolated
candidate count, and bridge count. Two supported clusters are allowed. If eight
supported non-redundant candidates are unavailable, the output abstains.
