# Deep-learning entry decision

Deep learning is not ready. Entry requires a completed deterministic and
classical baseline, stable grouped holdout, learning curve, tail-label and
source-family generalization results, adequate multi-target/relevance events,
complete rights/consent, and an evidenced benefit from representation learning.

The provisional scale guide of 5,000 multi-target effective records or 20,000
independent pairwise/listwise events does not override a learning curve or
rights failure. No neural network, cross-encoder, fine-tuned language model,
reinforcement learning system, or adaptive learned policy ran.

`DEEP_LEARNING_ENTRY_READY=false`.
