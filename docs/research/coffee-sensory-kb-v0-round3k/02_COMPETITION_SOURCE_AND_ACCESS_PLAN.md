# Competition source and access plan

## Authority and present state

The machine-readable source census in `db/data/round3k/` is authoritative.
It contains a planning inventory of 24 competition series and 50 editions. It
does **not** contain 24 acquired series or 50 acquired editions. Every inventoried
edition has `acquired_record_count=0`; the admitted database corpus contains zero
series, zero editions, zero observed P1/P2 records, and zero model-eligible
records.

Round 3K uses only official organizer, competition, results, scoresheet, auction,
or authorized-export routes. A public page establishes access, not permission.
The six rights dimensions remain separate, and `UNKNOWN` or `REQUEST_REQUIRED`
cannot be promoted to an affirmative decision.

## Priority order

1. **Cup of Excellence public feasibility.** Inspect official archive structure,
   stable lot identity, round and preparation lineage, explicit sensory fields,
   and source-specific rights before any import.
2. **Best of Panama public feasibility.** Verify whether official results and
   auction surfaces support stable lots, rounds, preparation evidence, and
   descriptor provenance. Do not treat a category narrative as lot sensory data.
3. **WCC public surfaces.** Use rankings, rules, scoresheets, and the Competition
   Body registry as separate source types. Registry bodies are not inferred to
   operate particular competitions.
4. **Organizer partnerships.** Golden Bean remains the highest-priority
   partnership prospect, followed by other sources in the request matrix. Every
   packet remains unsent pending user authority.

## Cup of Excellence feasibility finding

The [official competition and auction archive](https://cupofexcellence.org/competition-auction-results/)
exposes 168 visible country-edition labels. These are archive-index entries, not
coffee records. Seven completed 2026 program routes were identified. The
[El Salvador 2026 page](https://allianceforcoffeeexcellence.org/el-salvador-2026/)
provides public table metadata, but the inspected surface did not establish
lot-level sensory descriptors. The
[farm directory](https://farmdirectory.cupofexcellence.org/listings/) paginates
without a verified total, and the inspected
[Thailand 2026 representative listing](https://farmdirectory.cupofexcellence.org/listing/nw-phu-chee-deun-coffee-thailand-2026/)
showed rank, score, variety, process, and country but no sensory field.

The inspected [ACE terms](https://allianceforcoffeeexcellence.org/terms-and-conditions/)
did not provide an explicit research, descriptor-reuse, derived-release, or
model-use grant. Consequently, the public campaign remains `BLOCKED_RIGHTS` for
crediting records. No CoE page, file, lot, descriptor, or score was acquired into
the corpus.

## Best of Panama feasibility finding

The [official results index](https://bestofpanama.org/resultados) exposes partial
2021-2023 downloads. The official
[2023 auction](https://bestofpanama.auction/auction-2023) and
[2024 auction](https://bestofpanama.auction/auction-2024) each expose 50 unique
lot identifiers, but no stable lot-level sensory pages were verified. A
[2026 SCAP release](https://scap-panama.com/cafe-de-alta-gama-brillo-en-la-cata-nacional-del-best-of-panama-64-lotes-avanzan-a-la-ronda-internacional/)
states that 64 of 102 lots advanced and supplies category narrative; it does not
supply the required lot-level descriptor corpus.

Reuse and model rights remain `UNKNOWN` or `REQUEST_REQUIRED`. No Best of Panama
request was sent, no contact route was assumed from a WCC registry entry, and no
auction content was imported.

## Admission sequence

A future source may advance only through: official-source verification,
authorization classification, immutable snapshot and file hashes, adapter
contract validation, field-level quality audit, explicit fresh-preparation
lineage, duplicate/repeat review, and rights decisions. Only then may the
database views calculate observed and model-eligible records. Planning estimates,
page counts, auction lot counts, and registry rows never bypass this sequence.
