# Flavor-document definition

The Round 3J acquisition unit is a source-authored flavor-description document
tied to an identifiable coffee, coffee product, sensory sample, competition
lot, consumer tasting, or professional sensory evaluation. It may contain
free-text notes, CATA or RATA choices, trained-panel profiles, professional
cupping or competition narratives, product notes, review prose, or auction-lot
sensory notes.

A generic title, ingredients, nutrition record, preparation instruction,
unanchored education prose, country stereotype, chemistry term without a
perceived-flavor reference, or marketing slogan without sensory content is not
a qualifying document. One document may contain many occurrences; one
normalized expression may occur in many documents. Document, occurrence,
unique-expression, identity, raw-row, and effective-unit counts must never be
interchanged.
