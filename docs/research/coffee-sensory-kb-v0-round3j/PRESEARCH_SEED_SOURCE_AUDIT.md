# Round 3J pre-search seed-source audit

This document freezes the user-specified conservative decisions before global
search or acquisition. It is not evidence that the sources have already been
independently verified or admitted.

## Open pilots

Open Food Facts and FoodRepo remain metadata-only until bounded, filtered
coffee-product pilots measure actual sensory-description yield. Open Food
Facts may be accessed only through an official API, dump, or official mirror;
its ODbL/share-alike boundary must remain explicit. FoodRepo images are
excluded unless separately necessary and governed.

## Consumer data

The original Great American Coffee Taste Test release and all mirrors require
an upstream-rights and privacy audit. Sensory prose and preference fields must
remain distinct. Unnecessary or sensitive demographics are excluded; a
participant linkage key is retained only if grouped splitting requires it and
the rights/privacy review permits it. A Kaggle uploader's declaration cannot
license the upstream study.

## Evaluation and noncommercial references

FlavorReasonBench-Coffee is evaluation-only and cannot increase observed
document, language, sample, or training counts. The RoasterDB sample is limited
to noncommercial schema/yield analysis pending a compatible product-use
policy. Neither may silently enter an unrestricted corpus.

## Permission and commercial lane

RoasterDB full, Lightyear Coffee Index, Cup of Excellence, Best of Panama,
Roastguide, and Respresso require cost/yield/rights or permission dossiers.
Lightyear must not be scraped; auction sites must not be unrestrictedly
crawled; application APIs must not be reverse engineered. No purchase,
request, subscription, contract, or commercial commitment is authorized.

## Blocked mirrors

Coffee Quality Institute and Coffee Review scrape mirrors remain blocked
pending upstream content rights. Scraper-code or repository licenses do not
license copied upstream prose. Specialty Coffee India Set 1 also remains
blocked until its official upstream, version, and license are verified.
