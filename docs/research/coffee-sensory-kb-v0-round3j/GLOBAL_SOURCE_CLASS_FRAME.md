# Round 3J global source-class frame

The machine-readable registry is
`db/data/round3j/global_source_class_frame.tsv`. Classes A through I replace
regional quotas as the acquisition-completeness frame:

- A: open coffee-specific datasets;
- B: controlled sensory datasets;
- C: consumer taste tests and preference datasets;
- D: specialty-coffee product catalogs;
- E: competitions and auctions;
- F: licensed source-authored blogs and reviews;
- G: open general product databases;
- H: permission, partnership, or commercial-license sources; and
- I: community and user-generated content.

Each class must review at least ten named credible candidates, or record an
evidence-backed explanation that fewer could be identified. Existing regional
candidates may satisfy a relevant global-class audit once; they must not be
duplicated as new sources or independent families.

## Three-stage admission

1. Metadata and rights triage verifies official upstream origin, owner,
   version, license or terms, access constraints, privacy, model-research use,
   public export, and mirror independence.
2. A bounded 50–200-record yield pilot measures qualifying flavor-document
   rate, sensory-expression rate, unique-expression yield, duplication,
   metadata, language, and source-authored rate.
3. Full acquisition is justified by at least one material property: 100
   qualifying documents, 50 genuinely new normalized expressions, 25 new
   effective source-local samples/configurations, a new independent family, a
   unique method or register, or high-value relationship evidence.

These are admission heuristics, not scientific significance tests.

## Stop rule

A class may close only after its coverage gate and two consecutive targeted
no-material-gain batches. Global acquisition may stop only when every class is
closed, blocked, or saturated; no known high-yield rights-cleared source is
unprocessed; and all high-yield permission or commercial options are
documented. Candidate discovery and prepared requests are audit work, not
material acquisition gain.
