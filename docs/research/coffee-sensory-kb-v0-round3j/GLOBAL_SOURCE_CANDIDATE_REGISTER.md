# Round 3J global flavor-source candidate register

The companion register
`db/data/round3j/global_flavor_source_candidate_register.tsv` begins with the
15 required seed candidates. It is additive and does not overwrite or amend
the original 17-candidate register or the completed regional register.

Each row records source class, official and upstream origin, estimated yields,
access and rights states, privacy, model-research and public-export states,
approval boundaries, one controlled disposition, and its controlling
limitation. Seed values containing `TO_VERIFY`, `UNKNOWN`, or `UNRESOLVED` are
deliberately conservative pre-search states rather than verified facts.

Candidate count is a coverage metric. A candidate becomes an admitted source
only after the three-stage gate, and mirrors remain members of their upstream
source family.
