# Rights and request status

## Field- and purpose-specific rights

Rights decisions are recorded separately for public discovery, internal
research analysis, derived research data, model research, deployment or
commercial model use, and raw redistribution. Public visibility never creates
affirmative model permission.

The descriptor census report assigns its directly audited lower-bound set to
73 rights-pending and 230 rights-unknown assertions, with zero affirmative
model-rights assertions. Those are report-receipt counts, not an imported
machine ledger.

Every admitted Round 3M pilot assertion receives an explicit purpose-specific
state even when that state is `PENDING` or `UNKNOWN`. Such rows may remain in a
lawful research-staged universe but cannot become model eligible.

The merged 516-row review universe has 3,096 purpose-specific rights rows:
2,658 `UNKNOWN` and 438 `PENDING`. The 376 rejected candidates account for
2,256 unknown decisions; the live pilot maps each of its 73 singular-pending
assertions to six pending purpose rows and each of its 67 singular-unknown
assertions to six unknown purpose rows. There are no affirmative or prohibited
live rights grants. Decision completeness is 100%, but permission completeness
is zero for model eligibility.

## Organizer request preparation

`db/data/round3m/ORGANIZER_REQUEST_MATRIX.tsv` contains field-specific drafts
for ACE/CoE, IIAC, WCC and national competition bodies, Golden Bean, and Global
Coffee Awards. The drafts distinguish field authorship, observation level,
internal research, model research, deployment, derived-data publication, and
archive access.

The matrix contains 34 rows. Every row remains `NOT_SENT`.

```text
REQUEST_STATUS=NOT_SENT
OUTBOUND_DATA_REQUEST_COUNT=0
CONTRACT_ACCEPTANCE_COUNT=0
COMMERCIAL_PURCHASE_COUNT=0
```

No request was sent and no contract, credential, or purchase action was taken.
