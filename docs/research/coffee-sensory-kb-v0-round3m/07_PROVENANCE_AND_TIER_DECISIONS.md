# Provenance and tier decisions

## Decision rules

- Explicit judge or panel identity is required for P1.
- Explicit organizer-published jury provenance is required for P2.
- Producer, farm, competitor, or entrant sensory statements remain P3.
- Roaster or commercial sensory language remains P4.
- Public visibility and organizer hosting do not change evidence tier.
- Frequency values do not create judge rows or independent coffee records.

The Honduras 2017 explicit-jury route is the positive P2 path. Generic Peru
2025 and Mexico 2023 organizer fields remain unresolved. Colombia South 2008
frequency-coded fields remain unresolved P1 candidates. The AVPA category rows
are non-descriptors.

The live ledger's tier distribution is 73 P2 and 67 unresolved. The latter 67
are not counted as P1: 54 are frequency-coded unresolved P1 candidates and 13
are generic-field origin-unresolved assertions. No live row is promoted to P1,
P3, or P4, and no evidence tier is inferred from organizer hosting alone.
