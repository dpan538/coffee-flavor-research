# Open gaps

## Preferred coverage gaps

All mandatory freeze-coverage gates pass, but coverage is not complete.

| Dimension                              | Preferred | Round 3I | Remaining gap | Freeze effect              |
| -------------------------------------- | --------: | -------: | ------------: | -------------------------- |
| Contemporary tasting-language families |         5 |        3 |             2 | Non-blocking preferred gap |
| Governed unique normalized expressions |     3,500 |    2,996 |           504 | Non-blocking preferred gap |
| Source-authored `zh-Hans` families     |         3 |        2 |             1 | Non-blocking preferred gap |

The contemporary-document preferred threshold passes at 3,289 against 1,500.
The `zh-Hans` sensory-depth threshold passes at 249 against 200, so
`ZH_HANS_SENSORY_DEPTH_WARNING=false`. Four ranges now have cross-source
evidence, so `RANGE_CROSS_SOURCE_DEPTH_WARNING=false`. Passing those two
preferred gates does not erase the three breadth gaps above.

## Language limitations

The contemporary corpus is not balanced across origins: 3,186 of 3,289
qualifying evaluation documents come from the Cotter study. The Bollen and
Vezzulli records are aggregate sensory profiles, not free-form consumer prose.
Firstbloom adds substantial long-tail vocabulary but remains one historical
industry origin and contributes no new contemporary family.

The 249 Simplified-Chinese expressions come from two source-authored public
origins. They close the hard independence and preferred depth gates but do not
represent all Mainland Chinese regions, professional communities, consumer
registers, or culturally situated meanings. They do not establish preferred
translations or one-to-one mappings to the 130 concepts. Six unresolved
sensory expressions remain intentionally unresolved.

## Sensory and relationship limitations

The sensory plane remains federated. CATA selection, JAR adequacy, trained-
panel medians, descriptor frequencies, hedonic measures, and prose occurrences
do not share a common unit. Missingness and harmonization are declared, but no
future modeling protocol has yet demonstrated that the eligible partitions can
be combined without bias.

The Cotter acidity--citrus result is correlational and source-local to one
washed, medium-roast Honduras coffee under 27 batch-filter conditions. It
provides a second-origin evidence path for the preferred breadth calculation;
it does not prove causality, synonymy, or universal generalization. The target
membership stays `SOURCE_LOCAL_SUPPORTED`, the acidity-character range stays
`CANDIDATE`, all seven association ranges remain candidates, and contradictory
claims remain part of the evidence history.

The current totals of six source-local-supported and four cross-source-
supported memberships meet the hard minima but remain below their respective
preferred depths of 10 and 6. A seventh source-local-evidenced range should not
be forced merely for symmetry.

## Question and model limitations

Twelve question targets have independent research support, but
`QUESTION_USER_VALIDATED_COUNT=0` and
`QUESTION_INFORMATION_GAIN_ESTIMATED_COUNT=0`. Those zeros are truthful
boundaries: no real-human collection occurred and no adaptive questioning
policy was tested.

The 20 features and 12 partitions are prebuild registries. They do not contain
a trained ranking model, embedding baseline, learned policy, calibrated
probability, deployed inference path, or executed train/test split. Leakage
controls are declared rules, not measured future-model performance.

## Rights and availability limitations

Some governed sources allow public derived aggregates or expressions but not
public raw-row redistribution. Pseudonymous source fields and workbook metadata
remain external or excluded. Public release completeness therefore means every
decision is explicit and obeyed, not that every acquired byte is republished.

Future access loss or rights changes require a new release decision. They do
not authorize silent replacement of the v0.1.0 source snapshot or manifest.

## Recommended next work

The next authorized phase may design and evaluate the first model-prebuild
protocol against the pinned v0.1.0 views while preserving source partitions,
grouping keys, rights, and missingness semantics. It must separately authorize
any model, embedding, or human-study work. In parallel, a future v0.2 evidence
round can target two additional contemporary origins, at least 504 new global
expressions, and a third independent source-authored `zh-Hans` family without
changing the historical v0.1.0 baseline.

These are post-freeze known gaps, not reasons to fabricate rows or delay an
otherwise passing hard-gate freeze.
