# Round 3I executive synthesis

## Decision

Round 3I closes the mandatory evidence and language gaps without changing the
canonical ontology. The repository-contained database state is
`FREEZE_CANDIDATE_WITH_PREFERRED_GAPS`: all hard data gates pass, while three
preferred breadth targets remain documented. The exact promoted main commit
becomes `RESEARCH_DATABASE_V0_FROZEN` only when the external two-rebuild
attestation, green feature/main CI, and annotated
`coffee-sensory-research-db-v0.1.0` tag bind that same commit. The final commit
SHA is intentionally not embedded in its own manifest.

## Acquisition outcome

Four targeted batches admitted seven role-specific, rights-cleared source
records representing six canonical origins. Five independent language families
are new: three contemporary evaluation families and two genuinely
source-authored Simplified-Chinese families. Firstbloom remains one historical
family, and the Cotter relationship analysis reuses the same Dryad origin rather
than manufacturing another family.

The final relationship batch adds one `SUPPORTS` claim for the
acidity-character/citrus relationship using an independent Cotter reanalysis.
The membership remains `SOURCE_LOCAL_SUPPORTED`, every association range
remains `CANDIDATE`, and correlation is not represented as causation, synonymy,
or a universal coffee law.

## Freeze architecture

Migrations `000` through `044` remain byte-for-byte governed by their frozen
baselines. Forward migrations `045` through `048` add observed-language
governance, source/version and row accounting, immutable release membership,
eight approved current views, eight deprecated research views, model/run and
embedding guards, the research-database freeze gate, and exact-main release
attestation.

The non-self-referential freeze package is
`db/data/freeze/coffee-sensory-research-db-v0/`. It contains 10 deterministic
database inventories plus `FREEZE_MANIFEST.json`; the manifest SHA-256 is
`10ed5e29972082bc5046e6fb9c14be3f24b103a94a79c2482e5cd4819aa3991e`.
The manifest binds the verified starting source SHA and expected-state
checkpoint, while the annotated tag and post-commit attestation bind the exact
final main SHA.

## Boundaries and remaining gaps

Round 3I performs no ranking-model training, adaptive-policy training, deep
learning, embedding baseline, pgvector introduction, real-human collection,
question validation, information-gain estimation, product-frontend change, or
canonical-ontology change. The database is a governed research baseline, not
ground truth, training truth, a deployed model, or a complete inventory of
coffee language.

Preferred gaps remain: two additional contemporary language families, 504
additional governed unique expressions, and one additional independent
source-authored `zh-Hans` family. These are explicit v0.2-or-later acquisition
opportunities, not reasons to revise the frozen v0.1.0 hard thresholds.
