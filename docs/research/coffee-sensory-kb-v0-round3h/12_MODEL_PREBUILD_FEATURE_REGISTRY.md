# Model-prebuild feature registry

Twenty features declare semantics, unit or coding rules, missingness,
harmonization, and exclusions before any modeling. Seven are semantically
compatible, seven partially compatible, three not compatible, and three
source-local only.

Descriptor intensity, liking, and question response are explicitly not
compatible across sources. CATA selection, RATA rating, and purchase intent
remain source-local. These declarations are constraints on a future protocol,
not authorization to train or evaluate one.
