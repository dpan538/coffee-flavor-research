# Milk evidence status

Two independent sensory-outcome families cover coffee with milk or
plant-based alternatives. Gorman reports Canadian consumer CATA and liking for
dairy, soy, almond, and oat additions. Nguyen reports trained intensity and
Thai consumer outcomes for ten chilled, sweetened espresso–plant-alternative
conditions.

Milk evidence therefore passes the preferred family gate, but it must remain
partitioned by matrix, country, sweetness, temperature, panel type, and method.
Neither study justifies a universal milk effect or a conversion to black-coffee
scores.
