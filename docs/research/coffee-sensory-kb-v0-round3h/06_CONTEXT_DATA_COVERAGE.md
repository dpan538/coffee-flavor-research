# Context data coverage

Observed sensory outcomes cover eight preparation families and nine reported
roast categories or source schemes. There are 118 observed preparation × roast
crossed cells and 181 empirical coverage cells, exceeding the frozen minimums
of 12 and 120 respectively.

Every cell is backed by a reported source observation. Missing contexts are
not zero-filled, and source-local roast labels are not silently projected onto
a universal roast scale. Preparation and roast are future grouping keys where
their study designs require them.
