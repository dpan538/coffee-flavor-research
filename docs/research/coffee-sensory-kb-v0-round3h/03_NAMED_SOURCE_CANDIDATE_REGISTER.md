## Pending and rejected boundaries

Open Food Facts remains pending a content profile and separate ODbL partition.
RoastDB and LoffeeLabs require explicit commercial, derivative, machine-use,
and public-derived-export agreements with a defensible chain of title.
Chinese-Wikipedia revisions and OPUS GlobalVoices remain pending exact
raw-script and coffee-sensory yield audits. Request-only data, noncommercial or
no-derivatives licenses, all-rights-reserved publishers, unclear scraped
corpora, mirrors, chemistry-only data, dictionaries, standards, and synthetic
translations do not enter readiness counts.
