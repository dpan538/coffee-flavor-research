# Contemporary language corpus

Two targeted acquisition batches found no contemporary tasting-language
source that simultaneously satisfied content relevance, independent-family
rules, versioning, redistribution rights, and a lawful public-export path.
Consequently Round 3H adds zero families and zero documents. The governed
unique-expression count remains 1,777, below the 2,500 hard minimum.

Open Food Facts remains a possible future ODbL/DbCL partition after a fixed
dump is profiled. Sources with restrictive terms, unclear licensing, scraped
mirrors, or synthetic normalization were rejected rather than used to inflate
coverage. With no newly admitted corpus, concentration is not applicable and
does not trigger a warning.
