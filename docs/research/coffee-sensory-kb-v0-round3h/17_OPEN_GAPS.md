# Open gaps

The blocking data gaps are three independently governed contemporary
tasting-language families, 500 lawful new documents, 723 additional governed
unique expressions, and two independent source-authored Simplified-Chinese
lexical families. Two data-access requests are ready but unsent and do not
count toward readiness.

The next phase should be rights-first corpus acquisition, beginning with fixed
snapshots and content profiling. Thresholds must remain frozen; mirrors,
translations, derived duplicates, and generated variants cannot satisfy
independent-family or expression counts.
