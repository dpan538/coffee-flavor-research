# Source annotation matrix

The five outcome sources admitted in acquisition batches 1 and 2 are Iswaldi
et al. (ordinary-consumer RATA/liking), Vezzulli et al. (trained descriptive
panel), Bollen et al. (Q-graders), Gorman et al. (milk-coffee consumer
CATA/liking), and Nguyen et al. (trained and consumer plant-based-milk coffee).
Condelli, Heo, and Coffee Cuality add instrument constructs and relationship
evidence only.

All eight sources have an exact canonical origin, source version, independent
family, rights decision, privacy decision, snapshot, and public-export status.
The complete machine-readable annotations are in the batch manifests under
`db/data/round3h/`. Five outcome sources are CC BY 4.0; only public aggregates
or identifier-free source files were admitted. Request-only participant data
was not reconstructed.
