Engineering rehearsal may proceed. Recruitment, real sensory capture, and public human-data release may not.

## Privacy

The repository accepts pseudonymous assessor keys only. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, free-form identity notes, recruitment logs, and the identity-to-code key are excluded. Public metadata are minimized to approved analysis needs such as expertise band, interface language, and broad experience category. Optional demographics require specific approval, consent, justification, disclosure-control review, and a withdrawal policy.

## Consent and governance

Approval materials must cover caffeine and allergen risk, compensation, withdrawal, recording, retention, cross-border hosting, public data release, future machine use, commercial reuse where intended, and limits on withdrawing already published aggregate releases. Bilingual documents require human review. A data-protection and re-identification risk assessment precedes release.

## Release gate

Every release requires an immutable protocol and analysis-plan reference, manifest, checksums, machine-readable schema, data dictionary, license, citation, rights statement, split manifest, exclusions/deviations log, and PII scan. Raw identifiable material is never a release artifact. The project-authored schema and prose license does not confer rights over third-party inputs or participant data.
