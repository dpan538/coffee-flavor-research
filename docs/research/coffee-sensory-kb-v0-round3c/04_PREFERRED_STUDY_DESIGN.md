# Preferred calibration study

## Planned scale

```text
green coffee lots: 8
roast batches: 56
preparation families: 7
condition cells: 296
beverage replicates: 3
beverage samples: 888
reference assessors: 24
ordinary users: 240
ordinary-user presentation trials: 2,880
estimated collection: 10-14 weeks
```

Four core preparation families are crossed across all roasts; three additional
families use balanced anchor roasts. Stovetop/boiled remains a required design
review rather than an automatic exclusion.

## Statistical capability

Eight lots allow coffee-lot grouped development, validation, and held-out test
partitions. Partial-pooling models can estimate major context and interaction
patterns while retaining coffee, assessor, and batch uncertainty. Balanced
ordinary-user allocation targets at least 100 responses for major pooled
question/context strata, corresponding to an unclustered worst-case proportion
standard error near 0.05. Minimum-pilot variance, missingness, and intraclass
correlation must drive a preregistered simulation before launch.

Major costs are 56 roasts, 888 preparations, measurement, bilingual materials,
participant compensation, facilities, and governance.
