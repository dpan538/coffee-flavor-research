# Minimum pilot design

The selected minimum pilot is the 132-sample incomplete design in the
[protocol](../../protocols/COFFEE_SENSORY_CONTEXT_CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL_V0.md).

## Inventory

```text
green coffee lots: 2
roast batches: 14
preparation families: 7
condition cells: 66
beverage replicates: 2
beverage samples: 132
reference assessors: 12
ordinary users: 60
reference sessions: 72 assessor-sessions
ordinary-user sessions: 120 person-sessions
reference burden: 66 samples/person, six sessions of 11
ordinary-user burden: 12 samples/person, two sessions of six
estimated collection: 4-6 participant weeks plus 12-16 preparation days
```

Filter, immersion, and espresso are fully crossed with seven roast categories
for both lots. Hybrid/manual pressure, espresso + water, cold extraction, and
espresso + milk appear at light, medium, and dark anchors. Stovetop/boiled and
alternative milk are deferred.

## Capability and limit

The design validates physical feasibility, same-coffee comparison, roast and
preparation scheduling, repeat capture, context-prior construction mechanics,
adaptive-question simulation, import, and release workflow. Its small per-cell
ordinary-user counts and two coffee lots do not support a production model,
three-way lot holdout, or broad generalization.
