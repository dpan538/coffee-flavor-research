# Sensory protocol

Status: preregistered design; no human observations have been collected.

## Reference cohort

Twelve trained or demonstrably experienced assessors evaluate controlled samples in six sessions of eleven presentations. A sample is evaluated by six assessors. The protocol uses blinded three-digit serving codes, controlled serving temperature windows, palate resets, isolated booths where available, and randomized order. Assessors record descriptor presence/intensity, the five existing project sensory dimensions, confidence, and an optional constrained note. Reference responses remain individual observations; a derived consensus is a separately labelled analysis product.

## Ordinary-user cohort

Sixty ordinary users evaluate twelve samples each in two sessions of six. They receive consumer-readable bilingual prompts and no canonical descriptor answer key. The capture instrument records question assignments, permitted choices, response order, candidate-reference judgments, override opportunities, and completion timing. This cohort evaluates interaction feasibility and reference usefulness, not objective tasting accuracy.

## Samples and replication

The minimum matrix contains 66 condition cells and two independently prepared beverage replicates per cell (132 samples). Preparation recipe, water, grind, dose, beverage mass, temperature, time, equipment, roast batch, green lot, milk identity when used, operator, and deviations are recorded. Replicates must be independently prepared and may not be reconstructed from repeated database rows.

## Milk mode

Milk is a separate paired mode. One specified dairy milk and a frozen recipe are paired with the same espresso base; alternative milk is deferred. Black and milk observations are never collapsed into one label.

## Safety and deviations

Allergen disclosure, caffeine limits, withdrawal, exclusion criteria, adverse-event handling, and local food-safety procedures must be approved before recruitment. Protocol deviations are retained and flagged rather than silently corrected. Real collection is prohibited until the ethics, consent, and public-release rights gates all pass.
