# Round 3B executive synthesis

Round 3B converts two product decisions into forward-only, testable database
contracts: C0 preparation is mandatory with no user-facing unknown option, and
C1 roast has seven ordered project categories from extremely light through
extremely dark. The categories are product interaction positions, not equal
physical intervals, universal Agtron boundaries, or a coffee-industry
standard. The Round 3A five-level scheme remains immutable and historically
queryable.

Two exact CC0 Dryad dataset versions were acquired and frozen from an official
DOI mirror after their Dryad metadata and file hashes were independently
checked. They yield 4,817 raw context records: 3,186 consumer black-coffee
sensory-session rows and 1,631 literature meta-analysis chemistry rows. A third
candidate was refused because the referenced Dryad record was not publicly
viewable on 2026-08-25. No substitute was invented.

The held-out audit contains 17 of 102 project-authored bilingual and English
label cases. C0 family coverage is 1.0000 and C1 mapping coverage is 0.6250;
the latter deliberately abstains on three protected roast style/intent labels.
These values measure label normalization, not coffee flavor accuracy. Both
normalization sufficiency gates remain false because this is not an independent
ordinary-user sample.

The imported data do not identify sensory main effects or interactions. All
3,186 sensory rows occupy one normalized preparation-by-roast cell, and the
rows with varied context contain chemical rather than sensory outcomes.
Preparation, roast, preparation × roast, and milk-mode sufficiency gates are
therefore false. No production flavor coefficients, embeddings, Q1–Q5 logic,
or frontend context UI were created.
