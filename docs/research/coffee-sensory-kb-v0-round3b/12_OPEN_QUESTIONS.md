# Open questions

- Which candidate C0 labels are most understandable to ordinary Mainland
  Chinese and English-speaking users without teaching technical taxonomy?
- How should AeroPress be presented so the family is selectable while its
  legitimate polyhierarchy remains intact?
- Which sources support exact mappings for packaging variants such as `中浅`,
  `浅中`, and `中深`, and where must mappings remain source-specific?
- Can the inaccessible `10.5061/dryad.v15dv423h` version and rights record be
  lawfully acquired later without substituting another dataset?
- Which rights-cleared sensory datasets provide replicated preparation × roast
  cells and milk-coffee observations?
- How should measured roast color be calibrated to source-specific qualitative
  labels without treating the project categories as universal Agtron bins?
- What minimum independent sample and stratum coverage should promote C0/C1
  normalization sufficiency to true?
- After stronger context data exist, do context-aware exploratory models add
  held-out information over a context-free baseline?

These questions do not reopen the current product minimum: C0 remains mandatory
and C1 remains at least seven ordered categories.
