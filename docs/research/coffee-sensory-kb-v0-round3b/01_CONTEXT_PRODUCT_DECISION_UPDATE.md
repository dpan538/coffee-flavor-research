# Context product decision update

The current interaction requires a known broad preparation family before
sensory questions begin. It does not expose “unknown,” “unsure,” “not
reported,” “reported unresolved,” or “not applicable” as C0 choices. Those
states remain valid in `ref.context_value_status` and in observation storage,
because imported evidence may genuinely omit or ambiguously report context.

The current roast interaction contains seven distinct ordinal positions:
extremely light, light, medium-light, medium, medium-dark, dark, and extremely
dark. Medium-light and medium-dark are required product distinctions. The
ordinal positions encode only order. No interval or equal-distance column is
present in the roast scheme/category model.

This is a product constraint, not an empirical result. Research validates
whether source labels can be mapped into the required categories and may
abstain when evidence is insufficient. The governing rationale is recorded in
[`CONTEXT_INTERACTION_DECISION_20260825.md`](../../decisions/CONTEXT_INTERACTION_DECISION_20260825.md).
