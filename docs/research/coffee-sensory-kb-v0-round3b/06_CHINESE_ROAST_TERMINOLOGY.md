# Mainland Chinese roast terminology review

The review found recurring but non-universal use of distinctions corresponding
to light, medium-light, medium, medium-dark, and dark. An official-hosted copy
of Chinese agricultural standard NY/T 604—2020 uses `浅烘到浅中烘`; a Mainland
roaster product page uses `极浅烘`; and Mainland government product sampling
material includes `中浅烘焙`. These support retaining fine middle and extreme
positions in the target interaction, but they do not establish universal
synonymy or calibrated roast boundaries.

Reviewed examples:

- [NY/T 604—2020 official-hosted PDF](https://www.foodgacc.com/filespath/files/GACCREGISTRATON/Unroasted_coffee_and_cocoa_beans/NYT604-2020.pdf)
- [Fisher Coffee product terminology](https://www.fishercoffee.com.cn/%E4%BA%A7%E5%93%81%E4%BB%8B%E7%BB%8D)
- [Jinjiang government sampling report](https://www.jinjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zfxxgkzl/bmzfxxgk/scjdglj/zfxxgkml/202308/P020230815424576498041.pdf)
- [Chinese coffee research terminology example](https://www.spgykj.com/article/doi/10.13386/j.issn1002-0306.2025100033?viewType=citedby-info)

The project lexical cases therefore distinguish `极浅烘`, `浅烘`, `浅中烘` /
`中浅烘`, `中烘`, `中深烘`, `深烘`, and `极深烘`, plus `浅度`, `中度`, and
`深度` variants where review supports the intended project category. The map
is source-aware and reviewed lexical infrastructure; it does not claim that
all roasters or packages use every expression consistently. Trade names and
brew-intent labels remain unresolved. Chinese terminology review passes as a
bounded product-vocabulary review, not as a completed national lexicon.
