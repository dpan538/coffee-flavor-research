Round 3G expands evidence without expanding the ontology. The expected state
was frozen in commit `d5a2f895018dcc4dd6b22af4db6a99f8548a3cc3` before
named-source acquisition. The final database contains the same 130 canonical
concepts, 92 active sensory attributes, seven association ranges, 18 range
memberships and 18 question targets.

Six named candidates were reviewed. Two independent families were admitted:
the CC BY 4.0 [Liberica Coffee Sensory](https://doi.org/10.17632/m3n2gc4dv6.1)

files and 20 provenance-complete evidence claims result.

versioned coffee RATA source explicitly measured Smoky Aroma across nine sample
configurations. The seven ranges remain `CANDIDATE`. Contradictory evidence is
retained: the same study measures Smoky Aroma and Roasty Flavor separately,
and separately operationalizes Sour, Body and Astringent Aftertaste. No
cross-source, bilingual, calibration, question-validation or model claim is
made.
