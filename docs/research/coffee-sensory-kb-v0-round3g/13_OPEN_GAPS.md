# Open gaps

- Six high-risk English/Chinese wording groups still lack independent bilingual
  reviewer evidence.
- All 18 question targets lack ordinary-user comprehension and response data;
  information gain remains non-estimable.
- Six of seven ranges have no threshold-qualified source-local membership
  promotion; none has cross-source support.
- The Liberica study is one specialized formulation/protocol and cannot be
  generalized to coffees, populations or preparation contexts.
- Cocoa/nut/caramel, floral/tea and many fruit memberships received no direct
  admitted study evidence.
- The WCR Lexicon, Zenodo CC BY-NC dataset and CC-CEDICT automated download are
  not reusable under this repository's current public/commercial boundary.
- The two data-on-request sources remain unsent and unavailable.
- No real observation, user-validation, calibration, model, embedding or
  ranking evidence exists.

The recommended next phase is authorized human bilingual/comprehension review
plus rights-cleared, compatible coffee sensory acquisition—not ontology growth
or model training.
