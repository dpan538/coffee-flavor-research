# Bilingual terminology review

This round expands auditable bilingual evidence but does not complete an
independent Mainland Chinese sensory-language review. Wikidata contributes
versioned Chinese preparation labels; the earlier bounded Round 3B roast review
remains the principal seven-level terminology evidence. New question
translations are drafts.

## Seven-level roast language

| Project level   | Candidate Mainland expressions | Review note                                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| extremely light | `极浅烘`                       | attested as industry language; no universal colour boundary       |
| light           | `浅烘`, `浅度烘焙`             | register and producer usage vary                                  |
| medium-light    | `中浅烘`, `浅中烘`             | word order is source-specific; neither form is silently preferred |
| medium          | `中烘`, `中度烘焙`             | broad label; measurement equivalence is not asserted              |
| medium-dark     | `中深烘`                       | retained as a separate project interaction category               |
| dark            | `深烘`, `深度烘焙`             | does not imply a fixed Agtron or CIELAB range                     |
| extremely dark  | `极深烘`                       | candidate extreme label; independent market review remains open   |

These are lexical candidates for the project’s interaction categories, not a
national standard. The Round 3B review cites an official-hosted agricultural
standard, a Mainland product source and government sampling material. Round 3E
does not add a measured roast-colour dataset capable of calibrating these
expressions.

## Preparation and sensory terms

| English expression | Candidate Chinese expression | Regional/register concern                                                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| filter coffee      | `手冲咖啡`, `滴滤咖啡`       | technique and machine/filter registers differ                                 |
| immersion          | `浸泡式`                     | method-family wording may be more technical than consumer wording             |
| AeroPress          | `爱乐压`                     | trade-name transliteration; brand and generic-method roles must remain clear  |
| espresso           | `意式浓缩咖啡`, `浓缩咖啡`   | shortened form can be context dependent                                       |
| Americano          | `美式咖啡`                   | recipe and regional beverage expectations vary                                |
| long black         | `长黑咖啡`                   | familiarity is market dependent; do not collapse into Americano automatically |
| cold brew          | `冷萃咖啡`                   | not equivalent to every iced-coffee expression                                |
| tea-like           | `茶感`, `类似茶的`           | may refer to aroma, flavour, structure or dilution                            |
| jasmine            | `茉莉`, `茉莉花香`           | flower, tea and fragrance readings can differ                                 |
| bright / clean     | `明亮` / `干净`              | literal wording may not reproduce the intended sensory interpretation         |
| juicy              | `多汁感`, `果汁感`           | experiential metaphor needs comprehension review                              |

Literal translation is never treated as semantic validation. The new Chinese
question versions remain `CANDIDATE`; no item is `BILINGUAL_REVIEWED`,
`COMPREHENSION_READY` or `ACTIVE_FOR_CALIBRATION` on Round 3E evidence alone.
