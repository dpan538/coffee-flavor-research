## Throughput and procurement gates

- Planned physical matrix: 66 condition cells × two independently prepared
  replicates = 132 samples.
- Planned sessions: 72 reference assessor-sessions and 120 ordinary-user
  person-sessions; collection estimate remains 4–6 participant weeks plus
  12–16 preparation days.
- Green-coffee quantity must be calculated from roast loss, profiling waste,
  grinder purge, recipe dose, repeats and contingency before requesting quotes.
- The two lots must support seven separately controlled roast batches each;
  sample-roaster results cannot be assumed to transfer to a production roaster.
- Facility capacity, color-analyser method, electrical/water connections,
  refrigeration, milk segregation, consumables, staff, insurance, accessibility,
  dates, indicative total cost and lead times remain `QUOTE_REQUIRED` or `TBD`.
