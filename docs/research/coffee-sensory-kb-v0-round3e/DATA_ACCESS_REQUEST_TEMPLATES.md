# Data access request templates

These are drafts only. They must be personalized and separately authorized
before sending.

## De-identified source-data request

Subject: Request for de-identified coffee sensory study data and reuse terms

Dear [corresponding author],

I am documenting evidence for the Coffee Flavor Atlas, a research-driven
vocabulary and experimental-design prototype. Your paper, “[title]” ([DOI]),
states that data may be available on request. Would you be willing to share the
de-identified sample-level response table, coffee/preparation/roast condition
table, questionnaire or codebook, and exclusion/replicate metadata used in the
study?

We would use the files for non-clinical sensory-language research, question
design, and source-local quality profiling. We would not represent the data as
our own participant collection or combine semantically incompatible studies.
Please also state whether you permit: (1) research computation and machine
analysis, (2) publication of de-identified derived counts/quality profiles,
(3) redistribution of the original files, and (4) commercial reuse. We will
follow your requested citation and attribution.

No access or permission will be assumed unless you reply explicitly.

Kind regards,

[name and affiliation/contact]
