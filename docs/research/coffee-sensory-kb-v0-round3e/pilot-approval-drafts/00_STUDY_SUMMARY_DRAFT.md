# Draft study summary — not approved

Status: `DRAFT_ONLY`; institutional and investigator review required before use.

The proposed calibration pilot would compare controlled coffee preparations and
roast categories while studying whether ordinary users can answer bilingual
sensory-reference questions without forced-choice distortion. The frozen
minimum design plans 12 experienced reference assessors and 60 ordinary users.
Reference assessors would complete six sessions of 11 samples; ordinary users
would complete two sessions of six samples. The physical matrix contains 132
planned samples, not observations.

The study would collect de-identified response, timing, confidence, preparation,
roast, serving and session-order data. It would not collect direct identifiers
inside the research database. Contact and compensation records, if approved,
would be held separately with restricted access.

Primary risks are caffeine exposure, dairy or ingredient allergy/intolerance,
hot-liquid burns, sensory fatigue, discomfort from taste, and privacy loss.
Participation would be voluntary, with skip and stop options. No collection may
start until ethics/governance approval, facility and food-safety review,
investigator assignment, final bilingual review, and signed consent procedures
are in place.
