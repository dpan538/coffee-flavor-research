# Draft participant information — not approved

## English

You are invited to consider a research pilot about how people describe coffee.
If you choose to participate, you would taste blinded coffee samples and answer
questions about flavour, aroma, taste and mouthfeel. Experienced assessors
would attend six sessions of about 11 samples; ordinary participants would
attend two sessions of about six samples. Exact session duration remains to be
confirmed after a non-participant throughput rehearsal.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a sample or question, pause, or stop
without giving a reason or losing compensation already earned under the final
approved plan. Coffee contains caffeine. Some samples may contain dairy milk if
you separately qualify and agree to the milk condition. Tell the study team
about relevant allergies, intolerance, pregnancy/caffeine restrictions or
other reasons not to consume the samples. The team cannot provide medical
advice.

Responses would use a study code. Contact and payment details would be stored
separately. A de-identified dataset may be released only under the separate
public-release choice. Withdrawal is possible until the stated cut-off before
irreversible de-identification/public release. Study contacts, approval number,
complaints route, compensation, dates and withdrawal cut-off are `TBD`.

## 简体中文

我们拟邀请您考虑参加一项关于人们如何描述咖啡的研究试点。若您自愿参加，您将品尝使用盲码的咖啡样品，并回答有关风味、香气、味觉和口感的问题。有经验的评估员拟参加六场、每场约 11 个样品；普通参与者拟参加两场、每场约 6 个样品。每场时长须待非参与者流程演练后确定。

参加完全自愿。您可以跳过任何样品或问题、暂停或退出，无需说明理由；最终获批的补偿方案应保障您已完成部分的补偿。咖啡含咖啡因。只有在另行确认符合条件并同意后，您才会品尝含乳制品的样品。请告知研究团队相关过敏、不耐受、妊娠或咖啡因限制等情况。研究团队不提供医疗建议。

研究回答将使用研究编号；联系方式和付款信息将分开保存。只有在您另行选择同意公开数据后，去标识化数据才可发布。您可在不可逆去标识化／公开发布前所注明的截止时间之前申请撤回。研究联系人、审批编号、投诉渠道、补偿、日期和撤回截止时间均为 `待定`。
