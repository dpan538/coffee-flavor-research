# Draft withdrawal procedure — not approved

1. The participant contacts the approved study contact and supplies the private
   withdrawal token, not a public study identifier.
2. The data custodian verifies the token against the separately held linkage
   file and records the request date without copying contact details into the
   research database.
3. Before the published cut-off, the custodian quarantines and removes eligible
   research rows, derived rows and unreleased exports, while retaining only the
   minimum audit record permitted by the approved policy.
4. The custodian confirms the outcome and states any data that cannot be
   withdrawn because it was already irreversibly aggregated or publicly
   released under consent.
5. Withdrawal requests and exceptions receive independent review.

The final materials must name the contact, response time, cut-off and complaint
route. None is active in Round 3E.
