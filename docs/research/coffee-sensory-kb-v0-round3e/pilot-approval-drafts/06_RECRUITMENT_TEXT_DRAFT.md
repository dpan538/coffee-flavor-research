## English

Adults are invited to express interest in a research pilot about how people
describe coffee. Participation would involve two controlled tasting sessions

assessors. Coffee contains caffeine; a separately consented condition may
contain dairy milk. Participation is voluntary, and compensation is `TBD`.
Submitting interest does not enrol you. Eligibility, dates, location, approval
number, privacy notice and investigator contacts will be added only after all
approvals are in place.
