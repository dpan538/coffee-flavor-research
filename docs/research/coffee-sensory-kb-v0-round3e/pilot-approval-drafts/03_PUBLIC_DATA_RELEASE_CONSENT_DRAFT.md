# Draft public-data release consent — not approved

Public release is separate from study participation. Refusal does not prevent
participation unless an approving body decides otherwise.

- [ ] I agree that my de-identified sensory responses and non-identifying
      session metadata may be released publicly for research and machine use.
- [ ] I agree that my constrained free-text sensory notes may be released after
      review for identifiers. (Optional and separate.)
- [ ] I do not agree to public release; my data may be used only under the final
      approved private-research terms.

The release must exclude names, contact/payment details, signatures, free-text
containing identifiers, and any small-cell combination judged re-identifying.
The final form must explain license, repository, retention, downstream reuse,
withdrawal cut-off and the limits of withdrawing data after public release.

简体中文：公开发布同意须与参加研究的同意分开。公开数据只能包含去标识化的感官回答和获准的非识别性元数据；姓名、联系方式、付款信息、签名、含身份信息的自由文本以及可能重新识别个人的小样本组合不得公开。最终表格须分别征求“去标识化回答”和“经审查的自由文本”的可选同意，并说明许可、存储库、保留期、后续使用和撤回截止时间。
