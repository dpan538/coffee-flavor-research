# Draft privacy and data handling — not approved

- Assign random participant and session codes before collection.
- Keep names, contact, eligibility, consent and compensation records outside
  the research database with separate access control and encryption.
- Do not collect direct identifiers in tasting responses or question notes.
- Minimize demographics; every proposed field requires a stated analytic or
  safety purpose and small-cell review.
- Restrict raw data to approved personnel; log imports, exports and corrections.
- Scan constrained free text for identifiers before any public export.
- Preserve source values; normalized values are separate derived fields.
- Release only records covered by explicit public-release consent and the final
  disclosure-risk assessment.
- Document breach response, backup location, retention/destruction dates and
  data-controller contacts before approval.

Current gates: `PII_COLLECTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED`; `PUBLIC_PARTICIPANT_EXPORT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
