# Data license status

No human-participant observation is licensed or released in this package.

Project-authored schema, templates, and software retain the repository's
documented MIT/CC BY 4.0 layers. Those licenses do not grant rights over future
participant data, coffee suppliers' restricted material, or third-party
content. A real data license may be selected only after approval, consent,
institutional terms, source rights, disclosure risk, and intended commercial
and machine-use permissions are reconciled.

```text
PUBLIC_RELEASE_RIGHTS_READY=false
REAL_DATA_LICENSE_SELECTED=false
REAL_OBSERVATION_RELEASED=false
```
